Questions, answered plainly.

Still unsure about something? A short call is the fastest way to get clarity.

We're not technical. Can we still work with you?

Yes. Most of the people we work with are business owners and product leaders, not engineers. We explain every option in plain language and translate the technical decisions into business terms.

How do we know if AI is actually right for us?

That is exactly what the first conversation is for. We look at where your time and money go today, then tell you honestly whether AI helps, whether a simpler fix is better, or whether now is not the right moment.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope, but you will never get a surprise invoice. We define the work and the price up front, in writing, before anything starts.

How long does it take?

A focused first result often lands in a few weeks rather than months. We prioritize the highest-value piece first so you see value early.

Who owns the code and the data?

You do. All code, accounts, and intellectual property are yours from day one. Your data stays yours, and an NDA is available on request.

We already have a system. Can you work with it?

Usually, yes. We often modernize and connect existing systems rather than replacing them, so you keep what works and improve what does not.
